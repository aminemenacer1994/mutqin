# Mutqin landing-page visitor test checklist

Use this with 5 to 10 people who match Mutqin's intended audience. Record what each person says and does. Do not coach them or treat a task as successful unless they complete it unaided.

## Before each session

- Use a fresh browser profile or private window.
- Test on the participant's usual phone or computer where possible.
- Record device type, browser and whether the participant is new to Qur'an memorisation, returning, supporting a child, or an experienced Hifz student.

## Tasks

| Task | Prompt | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Five-second understanding | Show the landing page for five seconds, then hide it. Ask: “What is Mutqin and who is it for?” | Main idea understood, audience recognised, words they repeat. |
| Problem and solution | Ask: “What difficulty does Mutqin help with, and how?” | Whether they mention restarting, revision, a small range, or recitation feedback. |
| AI Recite | Ask them to find out what AI Recite does. | Time to find it; whether they understand it flags possible mistakes to practise again rather than guarantees accuracy. |
| CTA | Ask: “How would you begin?” | Time to find the primary CTA; whether the next screen matches their expectation. |
| Signup and first session | Ask them to create an account and begin a short session using a test account or approved test email. | Completion, validation problems, unexpected steps and abandonment point. |
| Waiting list | Where the waiting list is applicable, ask them to join with an approved test email. | Completion, confirmation clarity and any form confusion. |

## Score sheet

For each task, record one outcome: **completed unaided**, **completed with hesitation**, **not completed**, or **not applicable**. Capture the exact wording of confusion, the screen where it occurred and the time taken. Do not infer satisfaction or invent findings.

## Review after all sessions

- Count task outcomes by audience type and device.
- Group repeated confusion points by page area or step.
- Prioritise issues that block signup or a first session before copy or cosmetic preferences.
- Keep raw notes separate from any public marketing claims.
